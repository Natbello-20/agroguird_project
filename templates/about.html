About AgroGuard

AI-Powered Crop Protection for Ghana

What is AgroGuard?

AgroGuard is Ghana's first AI-powered mobile application designed to help farmers identify, prevent, and treat maize crop diseases quickly and accurately. Using advanced artificial intelligence and computer vision technology, we provide instant disease detection, severity assessment, and personalized treatment recommendations right from your smartphone.

Our mission is to empower Ghanaian farmers with modern technology to protect their crops, increase yields, and improve their livelihoods through data-driven agricultural practices.

Key Features

  • Instant Disease Detection: Take a photo of your maize leaf and get results in seconds using AI technology
  • Severity Assessment: Know exactly how much of your crop is affected (low, moderate, or high severity)
  • Smart Recommendations: Get severity-specific treatment advice that saves you money and improves outcomes
  • Weather Updates: Real-time weather information and disease risk alerts based on humidity and conditions
  • Extension Officer Alerts: Receive important notifications and agricultural advice from local AEOs
  • Local Language Support: Available in English, Twi, and other Ghanaian languages
  • Offline Capable: Works even with limited internet connectivity through Progressive Web App technology

Who Can Use AgroGuard?

Farmers

Scan your maize leaves for diseases, get instant diagnosis with confidence scores, receive treatment recommendations based on disease severity, check weather forecasts, view disease risk levels, and receive alerts from extension officers in your district.

Agricultural Extension Officers (AEOs)

Monitor disease trends across your district, send targeted alerts to farmers (by region, district, crop, or specific phone numbers), export farmer and scan data for reports, manage farmer profiles, track scan history and patterns, and provide expert guidance when farmers need additional support.

How It Works

  1. 1 Take a Photo: Use your phone camera to capture a clear image of the affected maize leaf
  2. 2 AI Analysis: Our AI model analyzes the image, detects the disease, and calculates severity
  3. 3 Get Results: Receive disease name, confidence score, affected percentage, and severity level
  4. 4 Follow Recommendations: Get severity-specific treatment advice (spray now, monitor, or prevent spread)
  5. 5 Call AEO (if needed): For high severity or uncertain results, contact your local extension officer

Our Technology

AgroGuard uses state-of-the-art TensorFlow Lite machine learning models trained specifically on Ghanaian maize diseases including Northern Leaf Blight, Gray Leaf Spot, and Common Rust. Our severity detection algorithm uses advanced computer vision to calculate the exact percentage of leaf area affected, ensuring you get honest, cost-effective treatment recommendations.

The app is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA), allowing it to work offline and be installed on your phone like a native app without needing to download from an app store.
