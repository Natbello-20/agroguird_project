Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

Introduction

AgroGuard Ghana ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our AI-powered crop disease detection application.

By using AgroGuard, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We are committed to transparency and protecting the privacy rights of Ghanaian farmers and agricultural extension officers.

Information We Collect

To provide disease detection and agricultural support services, we collect the following information:

For Farmers:
  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, location (region and district)
  • Farm Information: Farm size, crops grown, farming practices
  • Crop Images: Photos of maize leaves you submit for disease detection
  • Scan History: Disease detection results, dates, severity levels, and confidence scores
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates (optional) to provide localized weather and disease alerts
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device model for app optimization
For Agricultural Extension Officers (AEOs):
  • Account Information: Name, phone number, email, district assignment, employee ID
  • Profile Picture: Optional profile photo for identification
  • Activity Logs: Alerts sent, farmers managed, reports generated
  • Access Records: Login timestamps, dashboard usage patterns

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Disease Detection: Analyze crop images using AI to identify diseases and assess severity
  • Treatment Recommendations: Provide personalized, severity-specific treatment advice
  • Weather Alerts: Send timely notifications about weather conditions and disease risks
  • Extension Support: Enable AEOs to monitor trends, send alerts, and provide farmer support
  • Data Analysis: Aggregate anonymous data to track disease patterns and improve agricultural outcomes
  • Service Improvement: Enhance AI model accuracy and app functionality based on usage patterns
  • Communication: Send important alerts, updates, and agricultural advice

Data Storage & Security

We take the security of your information seriously and implement multiple protection measures:

  • Encrypted Storage: All personal data is stored in encrypted databases
  • Secure Transmission: Data transmitted between your device and our servers uses HTTPS encryption
  • Access Controls: Only authorized personnel have access to personal information
  • Local Processing: Disease detection models run on-device when possible to minimize data transmission
  • Regular Backups: Data is backed up regularly to prevent loss
  • Audit Logs: We maintain logs of data access for security monitoring
Your Images: Crop photos are stored securely and used only for disease detection and model improvement. They are not shared with third parties or used for any other purpose without your consent.

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We respect your privacy and limit data sharing to essential purposes:

  • Agricultural Extension Officers: AEOs in your district can view your scan history and farm information to provide targeted support
  • Aggregated Statistics: We may share anonymous, aggregated data with agricultural research institutions and government agencies to improve crop protection strategies
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by Ghanaian law or to protect our legal rights
We DO NOT: Sell your personal information to third parties, share your images with commercial entities, or use your data for advertising purposes.

Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information in your profile
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Data Portability: Export your scan history and farm data
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from non-essential notifications and alerts
  • Location Control: Disable location services at any time in your device settings

To exercise these rights, contact your local Agricultural Extension Officer or use the settings menu in the app.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide services and fulfill legal obligations:

  • Active Accounts: Data is retained while your account is active
  • Scan History: Disease detection records are kept for 3 years for analysis and trend monitoring
  • Inactive Accounts: Accounts inactive for 2 years may be archived or deleted
  • Legal Requirements: Some data may be retained longer to comply with Ghanaian regulations

Children's Privacy

AgroGuard is intended for use by adults (18 years and older). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact your local AEO so we can delete such information.

Cookies & Local Storage

AgroGuard uses local storage and cookies to enhance your experience:

  • Role Selection: Remember whether you're a farmer or AEO
  • Language Preference: Save your chosen language (English, Twi, etc.)
  • Offline Access: Cache app data for offline functionality
  • Session Management: Keep you logged in securely

You can clear this data anytime through your browser settings, though this may affect app functionality.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of significant changes through:

  • In-app notifications
  • Alerts from your AEO
  • Updated "Last Modified" date at the top of this policy

Continued use of AgroGuard after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data:

For Farmers: Contact your local Agricultural Extension Officer
For AEOs: Contact your district agricultural office
General Inquiries: Reach out through the app's support section

© 2026 AgroGuard Ghana. Your privacy is our priority.
